Based out of Media, Pennsylvania, Michael Risser is a licensed REALTOR® and Associate Broker bringing over 38 years of experience to the industry since 1988.

Milestones

Career History

Early Career (1988–1989)

Risser began his real estate career in 1988. He made an immediate impact in the industry, winning the "Rookie of the Year" award in both 1988 and 1989 with the first two brokerages he worked for.

RE/MAX Tenure

Prior to his current brokerage, he spent a significant portion of his career with RE/MAX. He was inducted into the prestigious RE/MAX Hall of Fame, an honor awarded to agents who earn over $1,000,000 in sales commissions.

Tesla Realty Group (Present)

He currently operates as an Associate Broker with Tesla Realty Group LLC, continuing his long track record as a consistent multi-million dollar producer.

Expertise

Specializations

Property Types & Clientele

His primary focus is on residential resales and new construction sales, handling a variety of home types, including single-family houses, condos, townhouses, and multi-family properties.

He represents both buyers and sellers, emphasizing his contract negotiation skills and custom marketing plans for property listings.

Geographic Focus

He serves a wide region across Eastern Pennsylvania, specializing in Berks, Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Lancaster, and Montgomery Counties, and numerous specific communities including Media, West Chester, Swarthmore, and Havertown.

Recent Markets: Media, West Goshen, Glenolden, West Chester, Downingtown, Essington, Lower Chichester, and Boothwyn.

Sales Performance

Over his career, he has successfully closed over 500 residential and commercial property transactions.

He maintains high transaction volumes. Recent aggregate data indicates his average sale prices range between $348,000 and $374,000. He holds a 5.0-star rating on Zillow.

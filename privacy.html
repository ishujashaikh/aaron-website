01 Policy Overview

At Aaron Peskowitz Real Estate (eXp Realty), we prioritize transparency and are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect when you visit our website, how we utilize that information to deliver real estate services, and the measures we take to keep your data secure.

02 Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when completing consultation forms, home evaluation requests, or contact inquiries on our site:

  • Contact Details: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number.
  • Property Preferences: Desired budget, home selling details, target neighborhoods, or timeline.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and page interaction data collected via standard web analytics.

03 How Your Information is Used

Information collected is used solely for legitimate business and client service purposes:

  • Fulfilling consultation requests and setting property viewing appointments.
  • Providing accurate market analysis and property valuation reports.
  • Communicating updates regarding real estate listings in Chadwicks, Utica, Herkimer, Sauquoit, New Hartford, Clinton, Remsen, Ilion, Mohawk, Little Falls, and surrounding communities.
  • Improving our website performance, security, and user experience.

04 Data Sharing & Security Safeguards

We Do Not Sell Your Data: Your personal contact information will never be sold, rented, or traded to third-party data brokers for commercial marketing.

Data is shared only with trusted technology service providers (such as web hosting, Cloudflare Turnstile spam verification, and internal CRM systems) strictly to facilitate requested services, or when required by state or federal law.

05 Cookies & Security Verification

Our website utilizes standard session cookies and Cloudflare Turnstile security scripts to ensure site stability, prevent spam form submissions, and analyze website traffic. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies at any time, though some interactive site features may be affected.

06 Your Privacy Rights & Opt-Out Options

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored in our communication records. You may opt out of receiving email updates or text messages at any time by contacting us directly.
